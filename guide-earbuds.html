BUYING GUIDE

How to choose earbuds without overpaying

A good comparison should look beyond the biggest discount badge. For earbuds, check microphone quality, battery life, fit, codec support, app support, warranty and return conditions.

5 things to compare

  1. Sound: look for a balanced tuning that suits your music.
  2. Calls: microphone performance matters if you take calls outdoors.
  3. Battery: compare total claimed battery and case capacity.
  4. Comfort: fit can matter more than a small difference in specifications.
  5. Warranty: confirm brand warranty and seller legitimacy.

Don't compare only the headline price

A ₹100 lower price is not automatically the better deal if the seller, warranty, delivery or return terms are materially worse.
