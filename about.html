OUR METHOD

A simpler way to compare before buying.

PriceCompare India is designed to help shoppers compare marketplace offers without jumping between multiple tabs. We aim to show the important buying information — price, ratings, reviews, seller context, delivery and value — in one place.

How comparisons work

When live marketplace integrations are connected, product data will be sourced through approved affiliate/API channels. Prices and availability can change, so the marketplace page remains the final source of truth at checkout.

What we optimise for

  • Useful comparisons rather than endless product lists.
  • Clear disclosure when a link may earn us a commission.
  • Value, not simply the lowest headline price.
  • Original buying guidance that helps people make decisions.
